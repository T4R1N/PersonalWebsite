AUTOMOLES

A cartoony autobattler RPG

Set in the world of MECHAMOLES. The game was inspired by a minigame in Shadows over Loathing where two teams go through. You had to guide them, but the minigame was short. I felt there was more depth to it to make it a unique, creative experience.

I've been working on this with my close friend, but we scaled too big initially. We're working on putting it on steam and itch.io!
